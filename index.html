Option 1

4 Days Tanzania Safari Cultural and Expedition

Safari Expert

Day 1: Airport Arrival & Hotel Transfer

Pick-up from Kilimanjaro International Airport (JRO) or Arusha Airport. Briefing and check-in at Arusha hotel.

Day 2: Tarangire National Park Drive

Morning pickup from hotel, depart to Tarangire for full-day game drive known for elephant herds.

Day 3: Cultural Tour at Lake Eyasi (Hadzabe & Datoga)

Early departure for Lake Eyasi to experience traditional hunting & blacksmith skills with Hadzabe & Datoga tribes.

Day 4: Ngorongoro Crater to Airport Transfer

Descend into Ngorongoro Crater for half-day safari tracking Big Five. Return transfer to Arusha/Airport.
